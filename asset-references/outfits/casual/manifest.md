# Outfit theme: Everyday casual

## jeans_regular
Straight-leg denim silhouette, mid-blue wash, simple stitch-line detail
at the seams (1-2 pixels, not overdone). Neutral, wearable with most
tops.

## crop_top
Fitted short top ending above the waistline, simple round or scoop
neckline, solid color or one small accent (stripe, small graphic dot).
Pairs with high-waisted bottoms.

## culottes
Wide-legged shorts that read as a skirt silhouette at a glance —
mid-thigh to knee length, flowy but structured. Solid muted color.

## hoodie_male
Oversized relaxed-fit hoodie, drawstrings at the neck (2 small vertical
lines), front pocket suggested with a simple horizontal line, hood
resting on shoulders/back. Muted color — charcoal, olive, rust.

## henley_shirt
Collarless pullover shirt with a simple round neckline and a short vertical
button placket (suggested by 2-3 small buttons or subtle vertical slit line)
at the center chest. Short sleeves, relaxed casual fit. Muted palette —
sage-olive green (#657947) matching the base character palette. Designed
as an isolated top-layer sprite that layers over the character torso.

## party_dress
Fitted bodice, flared or A-line skirt falling to knee or mid-calf,
simple thin shoulder straps or short sleeves. One statement color
(jewel tones work well — emerald, wine, sapphire) rather than a busy
pattern, to stay readable at pixel-art scale.

## evening_gown
Floor-length, fitted through the torso with a flowing skirt, subtle
waist definition. Rich solid color, optional thin accent line at the
waist (belt or sash). Reads as "formal/special occasion."
