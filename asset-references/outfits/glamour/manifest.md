# Outfit theme: Glamour & pop-icon fashion

Important: describe eras/aesthetics only, never a specific named real
person — matches the skill file's rule against depicting real public
figures. These get you the visual vibe without that risk.

## classic_hollywood_glamour
Fitted halter-neckline gown, ankle or floor length, single solid bold
color (white, red, or black are classic choices). Subtle waist cinch.
Pairs well with simple pulled-back or short curled hairstyle (separate
hair layer).

## vintage_pinup_style
Fitted high-waisted skirt (knee-length, slight flare), fitted top with
a sweetheart or simple round neckline. Polka-dot or solid pattern kept
simple for pixel-art readability. Bright, saturated but not neon color.

## modern_pop_stage_outfit
Bold, high-contrast two-piece outfit — cropped jacket or top paired
with high-waisted bottoms, one metallic or high-shine accent (suggested
via a brighter highlight color, not literal texture). Statement
silhouette over subtle detail.

## heels_footwear
Simple pointed or rounded-toe heel silhouette, thin heel line, worn as
a standalone footwear layer compatible with any of the above outfits or
casual wear.
