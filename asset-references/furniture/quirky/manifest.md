# Furniture Theme: Quirky-Eclectic

"Gallery piece" silhouettes, bold mismatched colors, and sculptural, unusual shapes readable in true 2:1 isometric projection with 1px dark charcoal outlines. Designed to feel like collectible designer home items that make an interior feel curated and eccentric.

## carpet_swirl
- **Concept:** Bold, asymmetric patterned rug with an organic swirling silhouette rather than a plain rectangle.
- **Palette:** 3–4 contrasting hues (e.g. cobalt blue, terracotta rust, chartreuse mustard, pale cream) separated by crisp graphic contour lines.
- **Footprint:** True 2:1 isometric floor tile footprint (~128px wide), sitting flat on the floor plane.
- **Silhouette:** Wavy, amoeba-like swirling edges with concentric color bands.

## sofa_wave
- **Concept:** Sculptural wave-backed sofa with asymmetric armrests inspired by post-modern Memphis / Art Deco gallery furniture.
- **Palette:** One bold, saturated color (e.g. rich burnt orange or electric cobalt velvet) with subtle pixel shading for plush cushion volume.
- **Footprint:** ~128px wide by ~80px deep, true 2:1 isometric orientation with 1px dark outline.
- **Silhouette:** High curved wave on one back corner tapering down to a sleek low armrest on the opposite side.

## lamp_claw
- **Concept:** Statement floor lamp featuring whimsical animal-claw-inspired brass feet, a tall slender sculptural stem, and a warm-glowing fabric shade.
- **Palette:** Antique brass / burnished gold claw tripod base, slender dark stem, parchment cream shade radiating a warm amber light.
- **Footprint:** Narrow base footprint (~48px wide), tall vertical silhouette (~100–120px height).
- **Silhouette:** Distinct tripod claw feet at the floor, straight thin post, geometric conical or trapezoidal lampshade.
