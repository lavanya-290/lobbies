# Asset References: Houseplants

This collection contains detailed visual design specifications for 4 decorative indoor houseplants tailored for the isometric world. 

### Global Technical Constraints
- **Projection:** True 2:1 isometric projection, matching Screaming Brain Studios (SBS) grid and existing furniture.
- **Views:** **Single isometric view** per plant (front-facing isometric angle, no SE/SW rotation required).
- **Outline:** Crisp 1px dark charcoal outline (`#1b1b2f`).
- **Scale & Dimensions:** Proportional to the ~91px chibi character and 128px tile footprint (compact footprint ~40–56px wide, heights ~52–84px).
- **Background:** Transparent PNG, anchored bottom-center for floor and tabletop placement.

- **Concept:** An elegant, thin-stemmed flowering indoor plant (reminiscent of a potted moth orchid or flowering jasmine stem) with slender arching stalks bearing vibrant delicate blossoms.

- **Flowers:** 4–5 five-petaled blossoms in vivid coral-magenta / soft lavender with bright sunny-yellow centers and budding florets along the stem tip.
