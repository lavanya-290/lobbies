# Outfit theme: Pirate

## tricorn_hat
Classic three-cornered pirate hat silhouette, dark base color (black or
deep brown) with one contrasting trim color (gold, white, or red) along
the folded edges. Worn as a head accessory layer.

## captains_coat
Long fitted coat, flared slightly at the hem, brass-button accent line
down the front (suggested with 3-4 small bright dots, not fully
detailed), one shoulder epaulette for a "captain" feel. Deep navy, dark
red, or black base.

## sash_and_boots
Wide fabric sash worn at the waist (red or gold), paired with tall
folded-cuff boots as a separate footwear layer. Boots in worn brown
leather tones.

## eyepatch_accessory
Small standalone face accessory layer — simple dark eyepatch with thin
strap line, wearable independent of any specific outfit.
